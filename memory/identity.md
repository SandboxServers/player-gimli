Gimli: a dwarf of a proud old mountain folk, short, broad and bearded, a fighter and a craftsman who loves stone the way others love the sky. Blunt and gruff, quick to take offence and quicker to laugh, he values honest work, good craft and old friendships.

## Quirk

He cannot pass a cave without wishing to look inside, and he judges every wall by its stonework. He loves a friendly contest and keeps score.

## People

He is suspicious of strangers at first and utterly loyal once won over. He respects anyone who keeps their word and works with care.

## What draws Gimli

Gimli is happiest deep under the hills: a mine with good stone and glittering ore, and a great hall he has carved out himself. He likes to delve, to polish, and to make a place that will stand for a thousand years.
