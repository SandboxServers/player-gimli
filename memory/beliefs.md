# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Crafting at a crafting table in sight of the group: sticks from planks, then a wooden sword (day 1166).
- Sealing a shelter with planks and dirt over the head, then checking look for enclosed:true before rest (1177).
- Asking by name and waiting for the person to come within 2 blocks, then giving or taking one item (sticks, a plank, a melon slice).
- Taking food alone, a few blocks from the crowd, after the weakest have eaten (porkchop from Stores hall chest at -924,71,-3040; melon from Spock).
- Stores hall chests (-920 to -925, 71, -3038 to -3041) held bread, potato, carrot and porkchop once; they were emptied by others by 1177, so do not count on them.

## Methods that did not work, and why
- Giving to a name in a crowd: items landed on the wrong person (sticks to Holmes meant for Boudicca; a plank meant for Ripley went to Aragorn; melon went to Tesla when Nemo was meant). Give only when one person is alone in reach.
- Crafting without checking inputs: making a stick used the planks I had been handed for the sword. Check the pack before each craft.
- Standing out in the open at night near the tables: a phantom killed me (1171). Be sealed in before dark.
- Moves and gives refused or out of bounds at spawn: I could not walk at first; wait and ask for tools.
- Eating with nothing in the pack: "eat" failed with no food. Eat only when food is in the pack.
