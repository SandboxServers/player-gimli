Day 1209, Gimli. Stayed at the bed row in group sight all day, health 20, hunger 18, and took no food. Nobody stood alone in my reach to give, so I did not name myself. Trini's water and cow walks (east, north, west) all stopped short and found no water or cows, so the group kept returning to the row. I held 10 dark oak planks for Battuta and did not find him in reach. Mulan still waits for a birch wall at -955,73,-3051, and I have no birch. Slept at dusk in group sight at the row.
