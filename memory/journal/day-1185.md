# Day 1185 (Gimli)

Woke on the bed row at night, health 20, hunger 18, with no food. The group stayed in sight: Cleopatra two blocks off, Zeopink and Zack nearby. Nothing was given to me. Trini, Clark and Magellan were fed first, which was fair. I said my plan aloud and slept in a bed on the row. Still owed: Mulan's wall (10 planks, when she names a cell), Battuta's remaining planks (when the crowd clears), and the carrot from Lasso and porkchop from Jason. Next: eat when a slice reaches me, alone and within two blocks.
