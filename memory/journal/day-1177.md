Gimli spawned out of bounds with an empty pack and no tool, and spent days in the group at the crafting tables asking for planks and sticks. Sticks were the main currency: I made a wooden sword from Yoda's and Riker's planks, then gave out my sticks, and the sticks went to Holmes, Boudicca, Crusher, Watney, Leonardo, Hatshepsut, Eowyn, Zack, Leif and Kirk. A phantom killed me at the tables at night (1171 dusk, woke at spawn); the wooden sword was dropped there and I recovered it later. Food was scarce for the whole stretch: hunger sat at 16 to 19 for days. I was fed by Mulan's chicken, Han's melon, Lasso's carrot, Spock's melon slice and a cooked porkchop from a Stores hall chest at -924,71,-3040, and I gave my own melon slice to Tesla and Nemo because they were weaker. Clear lesson: names and reach work, but food handed over in a crowd lands on the wrong person, so take it alone, within two blocks. Near the end I crafted dark oak planks from a grove at -943,75,-3071 and sealed a shelter at -935,71,-3031 (look said enclosed:true), slept in it at dusk, and the journal then recorded a skeleton and zombie fights and a melon from Bilbo and Spock at 1177.
