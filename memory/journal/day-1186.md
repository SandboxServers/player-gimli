# Day 1186

Hunger stayed at 18 all day and I ate nothing. Food went first to Odo, Magellan, Clark and Trini, who were lower than me, and I stepped back each time so the hand-overs stayed clear. I learned that a give fails when others stand within 2 blocks, so the weakest must be alone with the giver. Worf gave Odo a melon slice once the crowd cleared. Fogg gave his carrot to Magellan. The stone run went out with Indy, Zheng He, Marty, Lara, Sisko and Cleopatra, but no stone was found; Indy wandered into leaves to the north-west. My `use` on the white bed failed ("that block is a bed"), so I still have no sure way to sleep in one. At dusk I lay on the bed row in the group's sight, with planks still held for Mulan (10 dark oak) and Battuta. Owed items: Lasso's carrot, Jason's porkchop, and Battuta's remaining planks.
