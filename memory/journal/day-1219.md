# Day 1219 (Gimli)

Still at the bed row near -944,73,-3058, in group sight all day. Health 20, hunger 18, no food taken and none given. No water, stone or cows found by me yet. Trini named no leg for me. Mulan leads a short west cow-and-chicken walk with Quark and Harriet at morning; Chewie wants company to fetch his dropped pile. Ripley is hungriest of the group and holds carrots and melon for a lone, named caller at hunger 6 or below. I kept my rule of no walks alone and no night walks, and slept at the row in group sight.
